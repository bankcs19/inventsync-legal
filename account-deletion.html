Delete Your InventSync Account

InventSync by LoopDev Studio · Last updated: September 29, 2026

You can delete your InventSync account and its data at any time, either in the app or by email.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open InventSync and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

You're signed out right away and can't sign in again while the deletion is pending. After a 30-day grace period, your account and data are permanently deleted automatically.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app, email bankcs19@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my InventSync account". We'll permanently delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Changed your mind?

To cancel a deletion during the 30-day grace period, email bankcs19@gmail.com from your account's email address before the period ends. After it ends, deletion can't be undone.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (email, name, sign-in)Permanently deleted
Your warehouse, products and stock movement historyPermanently deleted
Your team: employee links and pending invite codesPermanently deleted
Your unlocked features (purchase records we store)Permanently deleted
Stock movements you recorded as an employee in someone else's warehouseKept in that owner's history, but no longer linked to you

Payment records for in-app purchases are held by Google Play, not by us, and are kept under Google's privacy policy. Deleting your InventSync account doesn't refund purchases.
