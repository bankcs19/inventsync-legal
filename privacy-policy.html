InventSync Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

InventSync ("the app", "we", "us") is an inventory management app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, or files unrelated to the app's inventory function. Apart from the ML Kit diagnostics above, we do not use third-party analytics, and we do not use any advertising SDKs.

How We Use Your Data

  • To create and secure your account (via Supabase Authentication)
  • To store and sync your inventory data across your devices
  • To unlock features you purchase through Google Play or the App Store
  • To let you invite team members to your warehouse (via an invite code you share yourself — we do not send emails on your behalf)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and inventory data securely.
  • Google Sign-In — if you sign in with Google, Google shares your basic profile (name, email, profile picture) with us. We don't receive your Google password or access other Google data.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — for processing in-app purchases. We do not receive or store your payment card details; these are handled entirely by Google/Apple.

Data Security

Data is protected using Row Level Security (RLS) at the database level, meaning your inventory data is only accessible to you and, if applicable, employees you've explicitly invited to your warehouse. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can request account deletion at any time from Settings, or by email. Deletion requests have a 30-day grace period during which you can cancel by contacting us. After 30 days, your account and its data are permanently deleted. See how to delete your account for the steps and exactly what is deleted.

Children's Privacy

InventSync is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
